Built Like a Vault

Your world doesn't get lost

A year of worldbuilding deserves paranoid engineering. The persistence layer was hardened end-to-end and is guarded by hundreds of regression tests.

Rotating backups

Every save rotates a three-deep, per-key backup buffer. Corrupted data triggers automatic recovery — never deletion.

Crash-proof autosave

Debounced saves with a hard max-wait, flushed synchronously on tab close — a browser crash costs you seconds, not sessions.

Multi-tab safety

Editing in two tabs? Realmweaver detects the conflict, blocks silent clobbering and lets you choose which reality wins.

Honest save status

Storage full? Saves fall back to IndexedDB and the indicator only reports success once the write is durably confirmed.
